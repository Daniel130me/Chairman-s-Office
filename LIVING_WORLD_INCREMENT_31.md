# Increment 31 — Travel and lifestyle operations
- Added persistent personal/business trips and bookings for flights, hotels, ground transport, experiences, private aviation and travel security.
- Trip planning has no financial side effect. Confirming a booking posts a debit to the personal account or company operating expense according to trip type.
- Cancellation is recorded; refunds are not invented and must be separately recorded.
- Added Travel & Lifestyle application. Live airline/hotel booking integrations and concierge/itinerary intelligence remain out of scope for this simulation-only workflow.
