# Increment 31 — Sequential, voiced boardroom discussion
- Board participants now respond sequentially, with each executive receiving the preceding turns and their own authorised business context. This allows meaningful challenge rather than parallel, isolated monologues.
- The meeting screen polls while a round is being generated so saved turns appear progressively.
- Optional device-native speech synthesis reads executive turns aloud, assigning available English system voices and modest voice variations by participant. This is not a substitute for native multi-party Vapi audio; real device voice availability differs.
- Meeting minutes remain grounded in persisted turns; unavailable AI responses are marked as partial rather than fabricated.
