# Increment 25 — Expanded company accounting
- Expanded the double-entry journal chart of accounts to cover cash, receivables, inventory, fixed assets, payables, tax, loans, equity, revenue, operating expense, payroll, COGS and depreciation.
- Invoice issue now debits receivables and credits revenue; invoice payment debits cash and credits receivables, preventing revenue double-counting.
- Payroll uses a distinct payroll-expense account.
- Added derived balance-sheet and profit/loss summaries with an explicit balance-check field.
- Added account chart metadata per company and helpers for future supplier, asset and loan workflows.
- Cash outflows now enforce sufficient available cash for every transaction that credits cash.
