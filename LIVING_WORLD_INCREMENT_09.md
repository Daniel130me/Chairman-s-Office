# Increment 09 — Internal investigations and invoice controls
- Invoice issue now applies transparent, bounded verification rules: same customer/amount within 30 days, or invoice >10% of current company cash. Flags create an investigation case and source evidence; no fraud conclusion is inferred.
- Chairman can open investigations, add evidence, and record substantiated, unsubstantiated, or closed outcomes.
- Case and evidence records are persistent, tenant-scoped and audited.
- Added Investigations app to the PWA.
- Remaining: full security operations, whistleblower intake, investigator assignment, chain-of-custody controls, evidence attachments, case access permissions, and independent review.
