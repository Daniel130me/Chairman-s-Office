# Increment 11 — Persistent external organizations and competitor activity
- Seeded a wider external network: investors, rivals, friends, partners, advisers, vendors, clients, bankers, regulators, journalists and suppliers.
- External organizations are persisted separately from the Chairman's owned companies, with sector, strategic intent, reputation and competitive-pressure attributes.
- Background market engine may generate a grounded fictional competitor move at most every 12 hours per world. Each move is persisted, creates a matter and event, and can request a Chairman call when a concrete decision is needed.
- Intelligence app displays external organizations and competitor moves.
- These are fictional market actions, not live real-world market facts. Real-world news and economic data integration remains separate work.
