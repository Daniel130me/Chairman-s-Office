- Meeting creation validates every attendee and writes an event. Meeting turns move the meeting into in-progress state.
- Added HR Director, Chief Security Officer, Internal Audit Director and Chief Commercial Officer to the founding leadership roster.
- Existing founding executives are lazily initialized as employment records with explicit opening salary assumptions; Board Chair remains a non-payroll governance role. User-created hires retain their entered salary basis.
