# Increment 10 — Evidence-backed delegated work
- Expanded the task engine to deterministic document-producing task types: financial, contract, investigation, employee, portfolio, meeting preparation and executive briefing.
- Each type has an explicit role allowlist. General free-text tasks remain assigned until a real handler exists.
- Reports are assembled from persistent database records and clearly label assumptions, unknowns and allegations. No AI-generated task completion claims.
- Engine processes up to eight supported tasks per world tick with durable status and document records.
- Added task type choices to Finance delegation UI.
